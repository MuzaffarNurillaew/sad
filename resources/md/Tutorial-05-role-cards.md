Role Cards for the Activity

Stakeholder A: University Registrar

•  You are responsible for course registration and student records.
•  Current system is paper-based, causing delays and errors.
•  You want an online system that allows students to register, drop, and view

schedules.

•  Key concerns: accuracy, student overload at registration periods, and data security.

Stakeholder B: Student Representative

•  You represent students.
•  Common complaints: it takes long for a confirmation, errors in course allocation,

and lack of confirmation emails.

•  You want a fast, mobile-friendly, and transparent registration system.

Stakeholder C: IT Administrator

•  You manage the existing servers.
•  You’re worried about system downtime and unauthorized access.
•  You need to ensure the new system integrates with the university’s existing systems

and runs securely.
