Business Information Systems 8

Requirement Gathering
• There are many stakeholders in every system.
• They can have different perspectives.
• Your job is to collect requirements that are:
• Complete
• Accurate
• Unambiguous
• No single method fits all cases. You should combine several
techniques.

Business Information Systems 11

Exercise: Role-Play
1. Divide into groups of 3.
2. Each group has 3 roles:
• System Analyst
• Stakeholder (Registrar, student, teacher, IT, etc.)
• Observer/Note-taker.
3. System analyst interviews the “client” for 10 minutes.
4. Observer takes notes about the communication style and clarity.
5. Each group will share 3 functional, 1 non-functional requirement and
a challenge they faced during the process.
