# Transcribed notes from `1-ceo-interview-notes.png`

Handwritten notes taken during/after the interview, transcribed and translated. Organized by topic; original mixed Uzbek/English kept alongside the translation where useful.

## Growth & feed economics

- 25 days → 1.1 kg
- 31 days → 2 kg
- 2 kg feed → 1 kg meat gained (feed conversion ratio)
- Feed stage pricing (per kg, as noted): starter ("350gr → start"): 8,000 so'm; grower ("rest"): 7,600 so'm; finisher ("rest → finish"): 7,300 so'm
- A "7.5 kg" figure appears crossed out/corrected — unclear what it originally referred to, don't treat as reliable.

## Procurement

- Feed is ordered on a 10-day cycle ("don → 10 kunlik zakaz"), with fast delivery ("tezkor dostavka").
- ~3,000 so'm per chick.
- Suppliers: Tashkent, Guliston — consistent with the "Tashkent only" correction already applied to the business case.
- Official arrangement: contract + credit ("Rasmiy: kontrakt + nasiya") — consistent with the financial Q&A answers (Q3/Q6: "Contract and credit").

## Director's own profit framing

"Foyda uchun: iloji boricha arzonroq xarajat, qimmatroq go'sht narxi" — "For profit: keep cost as cheap as feasible, and [get/sell at] a higher meat price."
