# Financial & Technical Requirements Questions (with the Director) — English Translation

This is the English translation of `1-ceo-interview-questions-financial-uz.md`, now including the director's answers.

## A. Financial side

1. **What's the rough cost breakdown per batch (chicks, feed, vitamins, utilities, labor, slaughter crew pay) — tracked separately, or lumped into one pDaftar number?**
   Answer: pDaftar keeps a separate income/expense account for each batch.

2. **How is "profit" in pDaftar actually calculated?**
   Answer: Profit = income − expense.

3. **When are costs actually paid — on order, on delivery, or on credit terms?**
   Answer: Contract and credit (deferred payment).

4. **When does revenue actually land — same day as the agent's Telegram photo totals, or is there a lag with any buyer?**
   Answer: In most cases, the same day.

5. **Does he know afterward whether one batch was more/less profitable than another, or is it all pooled continuously?**
   Answer: It can be determined afterward from pDaftar's history.

6. **Who funds working capital — self-funded from the prior batch, or outside financing/credit?**
   Answer: Contract and credit.

7. **How much does seasonal price swing move the margin?**
   Answer: Prices rise around holidays.

8. **Does the house-shop's direct retail revenue get recorded differently than the bazaar agent or Nurata seller's?**
   Answer: No — everything is grouped together at the batch level.

## B. Technical/product requirements

9. **What number would he want to see first on a daily screen?**
   Answer: Current batches and simple statistics.

10. **What one automatically-tracked thing would actually change a decision he makes regularly?**
    Answer: *(no answer given)*

11. **Would only he enter/view data, or should workers log things themselves?**
    Answer: Only the director himself enters data.

12. **Does a mortality tracker need to tie into the existing cameras/app, or is a simple separate log enough?**
    Answer: An Android app is enough.

## C. Still-open facts

18. **Is the "ready for main slaughter" decision ever recorded anywhere?**
