# Activity Diagram

The diagram follows the main slaughter of one batch and the sale of its meat to the Bazaar Agent. Five parties take part, there are three decisions, two of which send the flow back to an earlier step, and the work splits into parallel branches twice. Sales to the Nurata Seller and the Shop Customer are left out because the farm has not yet described those steps. The Delivery Driver has his own lane even though he is not an actor in section 2, because the meat cannot reach the bazaar without him.

![Activity diagram: main slaughter and sale to the Bazaar Agent](diagrams/activity-slaughter-and-sale.png)

*Figure 5.1: Activity diagram, main slaughter and sale to the Bazaar Agent*

The decision to slaughter rests on about ten birds weighed by the Director. Their weights are not written down anywhere, so a misjudged sample cannot be traced afterwards, and it means either paying for extra days of feed or selling birds below weight.

Once the crew has finished, two things happen at the same time, and both rely on trust rather than records. The Director pays the crew, the butcher per head, on a count he only hears, while the meat leaves for Navoi bazaar. There the price per kg is agreed verbally at the day's bazaar rate, and the agent and a Worker weigh the meat in parallel, which is the second fork. The Worker's tally is the only point in this process where the farm checks a figure given to it by someone outside.

The Director cannot work out what he is owed until the agent's photo arrives, so the money for each session waits on the agent. Whether the agent pays in cash or by bank transfer, the sale is recorded in pDaftar together with the transport cost. One session handles 1,000–1,200 birds, so the cycle repeats until no birds are left in the houses, and the profit of the batch is only known after the last session, too late to change anything during the slaughter.
