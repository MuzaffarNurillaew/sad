# Activity Diagram

One batch's slaughter and sale are depicted in the diagram. This process was selected because it involves four people, including three decisions, two of which require the flow to repeat earlier steps—and involves some parallel work.

![Activity diagram: slaughter and sale of a batch](diagrams/activity-slaughter-and-sale.png)

*Figure 6.1. Activity diagram: slaughter and sale of a batch*

Determining if the batch is ready is the first step in the procedure. The director weighs a random sample of birds, determines the average weight based on experience, and verifies the age of the chicks. The batch continues to be raised and is tested again later if it is not yet at sale weight.

The director notifies the driver and crew when the batch is prepared for slaughter. After slaughtering, scalding, plucking, and cleaning the birds, the staff calculates how many were processed. From this point on, two things happen simultaneously: the meat is transported to the bazaar while the director records the earnings and pays each member of the team. A farm worker simultaneously records each weighing while the agent weighs the meat at the bazaar. After that, the agent provides a Telegram photo of his totals.

The director determines the amount owed in kilos multiplied by the agreed-upon price after comparing the agent's total with the farm's tally and paying the driver. Both cash and bank transfer payments are documented in pDaftar together with the cost of transportation. The cycle continues till there are no more birds in the dwellings because the number of slaughter nights is not set.
